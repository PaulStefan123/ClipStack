CLIPSTACK
A small clipboard manager for your Mac
======================================

ClipStack remembers the last 10 things you copied, so you can paste any of them
again with a single shortcut.

  ⌘C           Copy as usual. ClipStack saves it automatically.
  ⌥⌘V          Open the clipboard list (Option + Command + V).
  ↑ / ↓        Move through the list.
  Return       Paste the selected item into the app you're using.
  1–9, 0       Paste that row straight away.
  Delete       Remove the selected item from the list.
  Esc          Close the list. Clicking anywhere else closes it too.

ClipStack lives in the menu bar (the clipboard icon at the top of your screen).
It has no Dock icon and starts automatically when you log in.

Requirements: macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer. It works on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.


INSTALLING (about 2 minutes)
----------------------------

1. Install the app

   Double-click the zip file if it isn't unzipped yet.
   Drag ClipStack.app into your Applications folder.

2. Let macOS open it

   ClipStack doesn't come from the App Store, so macOS blocks it
   the first time. Pick one of these options:

   Option A (Terminal, quickest):
     Open Terminal (press ⌘ Space, type "Terminal", then press Return),
     paste this line, and press Return:

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ClipStack.app

   Option B (System Settings):
     Double-click ClipStack in Applications and click "Done" on the warning.
     Then open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down,
     click "Open Anyway" next to ClipStack, and confirm.

3. Open ClipStack

   Double-click ClipStack in your Applications folder.
   A clipboard icon appears in the menu bar at the top right of your screen.

4. Allow it to paste for you (important)

   macOS will say "ClipStack would like to control this computer using
   accessibility features". Click "Open System Settings" and turn on the
   switch next to ClipStack.

   If you missed the prompt, go to:
     System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility
   and turn on ClipStack. If it isn't listed, click "+" and choose
   Applications › ClipStack.

   Then quit ClipStack (menu bar icon › Quit ClipStack) and open it again.

   ClipStack only uses this permission to press ⌘V for you when you hit
   Return. Without it, Return still copies the item, and you press ⌘V yourself.

5. Done

   Copy a few things, click into any text field, and press ⌥⌘V.


SETTINGS
--------

Click the menu bar icon › Settings… to:

  • Change the keyboard shortcut. Click the shortcut, then press the new
    combination. It must include ⌘, ⌥ or ⌃.
  • Turn "Open ClipStack at login" on or off.
  • Choose how long copies are kept: 1 hour, 1 day, 1 week, 30 days, or
    until newer copies replace them.

To wipe the list, use the menu bar icon › Clear History.


PRIVACY
-------

Everything stays on your Mac. ClipStack never connects to the internet.
It keeps your last 10 text copies in a file that only your user account can read:
  ~/Library/Application Support/ClipStack/history.json

It doesn't record items that password managers mark as private (1Password,
Bitwarden, Apple Passwords and others).


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------

"ClipStack can't be opened" or "unidentified developer"
  → Do step 2 above.

The "would like to control this computer" message keeps appearing,
even though ClipStack is turned on in Accessibility
  → This usually happens after an update. Go to System Settings ›
    Privacy & Security › Accessibility, select ClipStack, and click "–"
    to remove it. Then click "+", add Applications › ClipStack again, and
    turn it on. Quit ClipStack and open it again.

Return closes the list but nothing gets pasted
  → Same fix as above: check the Accessibility permission.

⌥⌘V does nothing
  → Another app is probably using that shortcut. Choose a different one
    in Settings.

Finder's "Move Item Here" (⌥⌘V) no longer works
  → ClipStack uses that shortcut. Use Edit › Move Item Here in Finder
    instead, or give ClipStack another shortcut in Settings.

It didn't start after restarting the Mac
  → Check that "Open ClipStack at login" is on in Settings, and that
    ClipStack is allowed in System Settings › General › Login Items.


UPDATING
--------

Quit ClipStack from the menu bar, replace ClipStack.app in Applications
with the new one, and repeat steps 2–4.


UNINSTALLING
------------

1. Open Settings and turn off "Open ClipStack at login".
2. Quit ClipStack from the menu bar.
3. Drag ClipStack.app from Applications to the Bin (Trash).
4. Optional: remove the saved history by deleting the folder
   ~/Library/Application Support/ClipStack
   (In Finder, choose Go › Go to Folder… and paste that path.)


Good to know: ClipStack saves plain text only. Images, files and
formatting (bold, colours) aren't kept.
